    How it works

    1. Pick a city

      Each company is pinned near the office you would walk into. Filter by area, team, or roles open to freshers.

    2. Open a company

      See every open role with its team, type and how recently it was posted. Save the ones you like with the star.

    3. Apply at the source

      Every role links to the company’s own job board. Track what you applied to and download the list as a CSV.

    Questions

    Where do the roles come from?

    From each company’s own public job board. A daily refresh reads those boards, keeps the roles located in Jaipur or Noida, and links back to the original listing.

    How fresh is the data?

    It refreshes once a day. When a company closes a role it disappears from the board, so it disappears here at the next refresh. The time of the last check is shown on the map page.

    Why is a company I know missing?

    Only companies whose job boards are open to the public can be read automatically. Many Jaipur companies hire through LinkedIn, Naukri or their own careers page. Those appear on the map as outlined pins without a job feed.

    Do you apply for me?

    No. You apply on the company’s own page. This site helps you find roles and keep track of them.

    Where are my saved roles stored?

    Only in your own browser, on the device you used. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, download the CSV first.
