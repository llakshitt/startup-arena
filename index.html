Questions

Where do the roles come from?

From each company’s own public job board. A refresh every 5 hours keeps the roles located in Jaipur or Noida and links back to the original listing.

How fresh is the data?

It refreshes every 5 hours. Only roles posted in the last 7 days are shown. When a company does not publish a posting date, the age counts from the day we first saw the role.

Why is a company I know missing?

Only companies whose job boards are open to the public can be read automatically. Many Jaipur companies hire through LinkedIn, Naukri or their own page. They appear on the Companies screen without a job feed.

Do you apply for me?

No. You apply on the company’s own page.

Where are my saved roles stored?

Only in your own browser. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Download the CSV before you clear browser data or switch devices.
